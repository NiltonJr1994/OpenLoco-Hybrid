OpenLoco Hybrid v0.7.0-alpha - first interior editor

Extract into a NEW folder. Copy your RCT2 Data and ObjData into RCT2 beside OpenLoco.exe. Keep Locomotion ObjData separate.

1. Start a single-player company, open Parks (Hybrid), select a regional model, Build park and Rotate gate. The entrance must touch a ground-level road. Check the quote before clicking.
2. Enter park opens a separate native INTERIOR window in the same executable.
3. Browse real RCT2 Scenery, rotate it and click grass in the independent 12x12 interior. Occupied cells and paths reject construction. Your company pays the displayed construction price once.
4. Build / Remove switches to removal (no refund). Close/reopen the interior: placement remains.
5. Save normally. Keep BOTH YourSave.SV5 and YourSave.SV5.olh. Reload and check regional parks, interior objects, ownership and financial counters. Loading must not charge construction again.
6. Corrupt/mismatched companions or missing required DATs must produce a visible error. Ordinary saves without a companion still load.
7. Existing monthly park tax/upkeep uses company Miscellaneous expenses and native inflation. No visitor revenue is generated yet.

LIMITS: this is an interior scenery editor, not a complete RCT2 simulation. Real static scenery and entrance sprites are rendered. Regional ride miniatures remain visual proxies. Paths are fixed. No visitors, operational rides/shops, TD6, SC6/SV6 import, transport destinations or park AI yet. Unsupported scenery animation/glass/overlay types are excluded. One object reserves a whole interior tile.

Small-scenery legacy price units map to Hybrid construction base units, adjusted by native inflation. Monthly tax: 98 + 5 per regional ride definition. Upkeep: 60 + 10 per regional ride definition (1900 base). Interior scenery has construction cost but no simulated operation.
