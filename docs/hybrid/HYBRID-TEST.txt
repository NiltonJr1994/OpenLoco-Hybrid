OpenLoco Hybrid v0.6.0-alpha - native park construction test

Extract into a new folder. Copy your RCT2 Data and ObjData directories into RCT2 beside OpenLoco.exe. Keep Locomotion ObjData separate.

Start a single-player scenario and create a company. Open Towns/Industries > Parks (Hybrid).

1. Confirm the window shows its version, registry status, model and prices. Drag the window: text must remain inside it.
2. Choose Classic Fair, Coaster Park or Family Gardens using Park model buttons.
3. Click Build park. Rotate gate chooses one of four entrance sides. The construction arrow marks the gate side even when the site is invalid.
4. Choose a 7x7 area near town. A ground-level road must touch the centre tile immediately outside the chosen entrance edge.
5. The full miniature appears ONLY at a valid site. Water, large slopes, industry land, roads/rails/buildings inside the footprint, bridges as access and other parks are rejected with a reason.
6. Trees are quoted for removal. Small height differences are quoted for levelling. Check the total BEFORE left-clicking. Insufficient funds must leave the terrain and cash unchanged.
7. Left-click once. The park stays, the quote is deducted once, the entrance is compact, and Enter park becomes available. Clicking the same site again must not double-charge.
8. Rotate the map and inspect the model. Ferris wheel, carousel, coaster train and slide have native miniature animations. Pause the game: animation pauses too. These are visual models linked to real RCT2 definitions, not the full RCT2 simulation.
9. Enter park lists real objects. Browse/Add object updates its object count and monthly cost.
10. Advance to the next month. Tax and upkeep debit the owning company's Miscellaneous expenses. No monthly charge occurs merely for previewing or cancelling.

FINANCE (1900 base units, adjusted by the game's native inflation):
Construction: Classic Fair 5000, Coaster Park 6500, Family Gardens 4500, plus native tree clearance/land levelling costs.
Monthly park tax: 2 per occupied tile (49 tiles) + 5 per object.
Monthly upkeep: 60 per park + 10 per object.
Tax uses construction inflation index 8; upkeep uses index 0. Charges use the same monthly company-payment path as vehicles; no automatic revenue is fabricated.

LIMITS: parks and their finances are session-only and are not saved in S5. No TD6 layout construction, visitors or operational RCT2 ride simulation. Terrain levelling is limited to 16 world-height units to avoid cutting large hills. Do not save/reload expecting Hybrid parks to persist.
